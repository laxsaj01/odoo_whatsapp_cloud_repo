Zero-Click PDF Invoicing

Upon posting any invoice, Odoo renders the QWeb PDF in memory, securely uploads it to Meta Cloud API, and sends it directly to the customer’s WhatsApp with custom payment portal buttons.

Real-Time Shipping Alerts

Validating a delivery order triggers an immediate dispatch notification containing carrier tracking references and instant status lookup links, drastically cutting "Where is my order?" support inquiries.

2-Way Discuss Integration

Incoming customer WhatsApp messages automatically create and stream into native Odoo Discuss channels. Agents reply straight from Odoo Discuss chatter, and messages are pushed to WhatsApp instantly.

4-Step Quick Setup (Up & Running in 10 Minutes)

Step 1
Create Meta App

Set up a free app at developers.facebook.com and copy your Phone Number ID and System User Token.

Step 2
Paste Credentials

Navigate to WhatsApp > Accounts, paste your credentials, and click Test Connection.

Step 3
Configure Webhook

Copy the auto-generated Callback URL & Verify Token into your Meta app dashboard.

Step 4
Sync & Automate

Click Sync Meta Templates and enable zero-click automations in Settings. You're done!
